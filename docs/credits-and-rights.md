# Credits, project history and rights notes

## Project history

MyPod is an independent web edition and fork of [ClassiPod by Aditya R and its
contributors](https://github.com/adeeteya/Classipod), based on upstream commit
[`772a6d3`](https://github.com/adeeteya/Classipod/commit/772a6d3). The personal
edition was developed under the direction of [nivednb](https://github.com/nivednb),
with AI-assisted implementation and iterative user feedback and testing.
The [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and [commit history](https://github.com/nivednb/MyPod/commits/master/)
document the changes. Commit author labels record Git metadata; they do not
alone determine legal authorship.

Highlights include browser-based music and playlist imports, offline app shell,
metadata caching, visual themes and customizable wheels, language grouping,
Brick, Snake, 2048 and Solitaire, and a configurable Now Playing delay.

## Questions to review before a commercial or native release

- The inherited [terms and conditions](../terms-and-conditions.md) describe
  Aditya's service and contain restrictions that appear inconsistent with the
  root open-source license. Their legal interaction has not been resolved.
- Verify provenance and permitted redistribution for the bundled Helvetica
  font files, click sound, icons, and remote demo music and artwork; the code
  license alone does not establish those rights. Pixelify Sans carries its own
  [OFL notice](../assets/fonts/PixelifySans-OFL.txt).
- Review the name, icon and iPod-inspired appearance for trademark, design and
  platform-review concerns. The upstream software license does not grant Apple
  trademark rights or imply endorsement by Apple or the original project.
- Create a release-specific inventory of direct and transitive dependencies and
  notices; the existing files are not a completed legal clearance.
- GitHub Pages is for the current static web edition; evaluate hosting terms,
  privacy disclosures, platform policies and payment requirements if the
  project becomes commercial.
- Existing public versions and licenses cannot simply be recalled by changing
  future notices. Ownership and attribution terms for new contributions require
  case-specific review, including contributions made with AI assistance.

These are unresolved review items, not a finding of infringement or a legal
opinion. No future rights or attribution obligations are asserted here.
